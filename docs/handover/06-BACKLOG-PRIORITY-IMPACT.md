# 06 — Backlog by Priority and Impact

> Sources: the 2026-07-20 security audit (27 findings — 3 critical, 10 high, 7 medium, 7
> low), the two 2026-07-20 mobile audits (web responsiveness and Expo store-readiness), the
> production bug batch of 2026-07-21, the outstanding business decisions from
> [`docs/OPEN_ITEMS.md`](../OPEN_ITEMS.md), and four gaps found while assembling this

> **Impact** is what breaks or is lost if it stays undone. **Effort** is a rough size:
> S is under a day, M is a few days, L is a week or more.

## Suggested sequence

1. **Week 1** — get access working end to end, then do P0-1 and P0-5. Rotation and error
   visibility are the two things that make everything afterwards safer.
2. **Week 2** — P0-2, P0-3, P0-4. All small, all high-consequence.
3. **Weeks 3–4** — P1-4, P1-8, P1-9, P1-2, P1-6. Cheap items that make production
   observable and complete the notification surface.
4. **Month 2** — P1-1, P1-3, P1-5, and P1-7 once the business completes Exotel KYC.
5. **Month 3 onwards** — the P2 band, sequenced against whatever the business decides on
   BIZ-1, since that determines whether payments work becomes urgent.

Push BIZ-1 and BIZ-2 in your first conversation with Rajesh. BIZ-1 gates revenue and BIZ-2
is a legal exposure that grows the longer it sits.
